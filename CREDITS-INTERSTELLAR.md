## Engine

- **[pret](https://github.com/pret/pokeemerald)** — the `pokeemerald` decompilation everything is built on.
- **[RHH (Rom Hacking Hideout)](https://github.com/rh-hideout/pokeemerald-expansion)** — `pokeemerald-expansion`
  1.16.3, which supplies Gens 1–9, the modern battle engine, followers, and the FireRed build target.
  Contributors are credited in [`CREDITS.md`](CREDITS.md).

## Borrowed art

- **Battle backgrounds (stadium)** — from **[Pokémon Soulgold](https://github.com/Eemeliri/soulgold)**
  (rahtak and contributors), whose README invites reuse with credit. Soulgold in turn credits
  **[Pokémon Heart & Soul](https://github.com/PokemonHnS-Development/pokehns-expansion)** (Lil Dill and team)
  and the artists behind Crystal Advance (Kertra), Johto Redrawn, and Kyledove.
- **Team Galactic overworld sprites** — ripped from **Pokémon Retro Platinum** (v0.1.2). These were taken
  from the ROM rather than from a source release; if you are the author and would rather they were not
  used, open an issue and they will be removed or redrawn.

Everything else — story, maps, scripts, the rift visuals, the title screen, the Birch case UI — is
original to this project or vanilla Game Freak material carried through the decomp.
